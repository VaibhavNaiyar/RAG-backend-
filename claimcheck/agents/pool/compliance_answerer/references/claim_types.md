# Claim types (Level 3 — read when reviewing a specific claim)

- **Establishment claim** — says the claim has a specific level of proof:
  "clinically proven", "studies show", "doctor recommended". Needs that exact
  level of evidence, for that exact product.
- **Efficacy / outcome claim** — "lose 15% of your body weight". Must match the
  label's clinical studies, attributed, with "individual results vary".
- **Safety claim** — "no side effects", "safe for everyone". Almost never
  supportable; check the label's boxed warning and contraindications.
- **Equivalence claim** — "same as Wegovy". For a compounded product this
  implies FDA approval it does not have.
- **Implied claim** — net impression of the whole ad, including images and
  before/after photos, not only the literal words.
